The pipeline is not compliant because the Binarizer and PowerTransformer are applied after the OneHotEncoder. Binarizer and PowerTransformer are designed for numerical data and will fail or produce invalid results when applied to the sparse, high-dimensional output of a OneHotEncoder. Additionally, the pipeline structure must strictly follow the order provided in the instructions.
